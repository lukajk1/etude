# Demo music credits

The compositions are all in the public domain. These MIDI files come from the
[Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/), a volunteer library of free sheet music.
Each file keeps the license it was published under. Files were renamed, and only the
movements used here were kept; the notes are unchanged. Etude replaces the files'
velocities (except the Rachmaninoff, which keeps its own dynamics) and, for Moonlight III,
the tempo when it opens them. Rachmaninoff died in 1943; the prelude was published in 1904.
